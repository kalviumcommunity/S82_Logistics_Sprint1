# CHURN REDUCTION INITIATIVE

## Executive Summary

### Situation
Customer churn is our leading revenue loss driver, costing $2M annually. The current churn rate is 7% vs our industry average of 4%. We analyzed root causes and identified a high-impact opportunity to plug this leak.

### Key Findings
- **Support speed directly impacts retention**: Customers receiving support within 2 hours churn at 3%. Customers waiting >24 hours churn at 12% (a 4x difference).
- **Current process is the bottleneck**: The current support team averages a 6-hour response time, which is our primary retention failure point.
- **High-value risk**: High-value customers (>$10K annual spend) are highly sensitive to support speed and churn at 15% when support is slow.

### Business Risks
- **Revenue at Risk**: At the current 7% churn rate, we lose $2M annually. The high-value customer segment is especially vulnerable.
- **Competitive Vulnerability**: Better-supported competitors are actively recruiting our dissatisfied customers.
- **Trend**: Support ticket volume is up 40% YoY while response times have degraded. Without action, churn will worsen.

### Recommendations
1. **Hire 2 Support Engineers** (Cost: $200K/year)
   - Reduces average response time from 6 hours to <2 hours
   - Expected impact: Recover $400K in churn reduction (ROI: 2x within the first year)
2. **Implement Response Time SLA** (Cost: $0)
   - Document a <2 hour target and track daily to create accountability
   - Implementation: Jan 1
3. **Prioritize High-Value Customers** (Cost: $50K engineering)
   - Route $10K+/year customers to a dedicated support lane
   - Expected impact: Reduce high-value churn by 50%
   - Timeline: Feb 1

### Decision Needed
Approve the hiring budget ($200K) and engineering resources ($50K) by Dec 15. The projected ROI is a $400K churn reduction in year 1.
