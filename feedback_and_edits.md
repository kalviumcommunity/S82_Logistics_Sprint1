# Feedback and Edits

## Peer Review Feedback
The narrative was shared with a peer outside the data team. They were asked the three clarity questions:

1. **What is the main finding?** 
   - *Peer's Answer:* Support response time strongly correlates with customer churn. Slower responses lead to more churn.
2. **What should we do about it?** 
   - *Peer's Answer:* Hire more support staff to decrease wait times, set a 2-hour SLA, and prioritize high-value customers.
3. **Did anything confuse you?** 
   - *Peer's Answer:* Initially, it wasn't explicitly clear *why* the response time SLA alone would help if we didn't have the staff. Also, the phrasing around the business impact felt slightly disconnected from the costs.

## Edits Made Based on Feedback
- Added the specific context that measurement (SLA) creates accountability which drives down the average response time.
- Clarified the cost-benefit analysis by explicitly stating that hiring 2 engineers costs $200K but recovers $400K, making the ROI obvious to leadership.
- Adjusted phrasing to ensure zero technical jargon (e.g., removed any implied statistical significance phrasing and replaced it with "the pattern is real and strong").
