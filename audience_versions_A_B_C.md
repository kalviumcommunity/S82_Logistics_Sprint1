# Audience Adjusted Communication

## Version A: For Board of Directors
Our current customer churn rate of 7% is costing the company $2M annually. We have identified slow support response times as the root cause. By investing $250K to expand our support capacity and implement dedicated routing, we expect to recover $400K in revenue within the first year, yielding an immediate positive ROI and protecting our most valuable enterprise accounts. Approve budget by Dec 15?

## Version B: For Operations Team
We are launching a new initiative to reduce our average support response time from 6 hours to under 2 hours. This requires hiring 2 additional support FTEs and implementing strict SLA tracking dashboards by Jan 1. Additionally, we will introduce queue prioritization routing logic for high-value customers by Feb 1 to ensure our largest accounts receive immediate attention. Let's review the hiring plan.

## Version C: For Support Team
We know ticket volumes have been overwhelming lately, and we are hiring 2 additional team members to help reduce your workload! With this new capacity, we are introducing a new SLA target of 2-hour responses. To help you manage this efficiently, you will receive new priority routing tools so you can easily identify and assist high-value customers without having to manually sift through the queue. Training begins Feb 1.
